## Paper instructions printed in this PDF

- Total: 75 marks; objective 15 + subjective 60.
- Duration: 2 hours 30 minutes; objective 20 minutes, subjective 2 hours 10 minutes.
- Q1: 15 MCQs. One each from Chapters 1, 2, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13; two each from Chapters 3, 4 and 6.
- Q2: offer 9 short questions; attempt 6. Two each from Chapters 1–4 and one from Chapter 5.
- Q3: offer 9 short questions; attempt 6. Three each from Chapters 6, 7 and 9.
- Q4: offer 9 short questions; attempt 6. Two each from Chapters 10, 11 and 13; three from Chapter 12.
- Q5: two 4-mark parts from Chapters 1 and 2. Q6: two 4-mark parts from Chapters 3 and 4. Q7: two 4-mark parts from Chapters 5 and 6. Attempt any two of Q5–Q7 (16 marks).
- Q8: part (a) from Chapter 7 OR 10; part (b) from Chapter 9. Q9: part (a) from Chapter 12; part (b) from Chapter 11 OR 13. Attempt one of Q8–Q9 (8 marks).
