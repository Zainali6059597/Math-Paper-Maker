Math Paper Maker — bundled offline content packs

The app imports every *.json package in this directory into its on-device Room database on first launch. Core content, paper generation, and PDF export do not require internet access or sign-in.

Current Grade 9 Mathematics packs (session 2026–27):
- Punjab: PECTAA Notification 1238, dated 6 November 2026; applies across Punjab BISEs.
- Punjab (Matric-Tech): PECTAA Notification 1253, dated 14 November 2026; explicitly scoped to Matric-Tech.

Each current pack contains 47 independently authored practice questions with suggested answers and a 75-mark pattern mapped to its official pairing scheme. The question wording is original and is not copied from the official model paper.

IMPORTANT:
- Imported sources, patterns, and questions remain NEEDS_REVIEW. The app never promotes them to official status automatically.
- Practice exports must be clearly marked as practice/unverified. Official-mode export requires independent human review and approval.
- Verify every question, answer, excluded topic, and board/session mapping with the current official textbook and notification before classroom or examination use.
- Do not reuse question IDs, source IDs, pattern IDs, or semantic fingerprints across packages.
- Use UTF-8 JSON and the schema supported by PackageImporter (currently schema versions 1–5).
- Never put credentials, private data, or network-dependent assets in these packs.
